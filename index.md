This series of workshops will introduce telemetry users to some analysis pathways in the R programming language, popular in the field of biologging.

Sessions will include:
- Working with Public OTN Data - by Dheemanth Kumawat
- Animations of Animal Movements - by Wesley Greentree
- Tips and Tricks for Visual Storytelling - by Megan Shier
- Animal Geolocation - by Edward Lavender
- Beginner Spatiotemportal Modelling with GAMs - by Caitlin Bate
- Leveraging YAPS - by Jonathan Rodemann

If you have questions about this curriculum, please reach out to otndc@dal.ca
