In an increasingly visual world, making sure your images have a strong story is essential to engaging with new and non-scientific audiences. It's easy to fall into default patterns if you don't have the proper groundwork for creativity to thrive. This can leave you with photographs that feel flat and the message you are trying to convey overlooked. Luckily, there are techniques to help you step out of simple documentation and move toward visual storytelling, creating images that resonate with people. 

In this workshop, we will walk through the visual elements that create emotional impact and common mistakes that can prevent your photographs from connecting. You will try out exercises for combatting creative ruts, experiment with sequencing images to tell engaging stories, and learn what's actually important when building your own creative practice. 

Attendees should bring a camera to fully participate in the activities (phone cameras are fine).
